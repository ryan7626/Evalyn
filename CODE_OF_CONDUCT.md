# Code of conduct

Evalyn contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation
welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free regardless of background, identity,
experience, or viewpoint.

Be constructive, assume good faith, focus criticism on the work, respect
privacy, and do not publish another person’s private information. Harassment,
threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, and sustained
disruption are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
exclude participants whose behavior harms the community. Report conduct issues
privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and
fairly, with action proportionate to impact and context.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing
the project.
